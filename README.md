## Investigation Methodology

My approach to cybersecurity investigations follows five steps:

1. Understand the incident scenario and identify the investigation objectives.
2. Examine available evidence, including logs, email headers, and other artifacts.
3. Research unfamiliar commands, tools, and attack techniques.
4. Identify suspicious activity and determine what the evidence supports.
5. Document findings and lessons learned.
